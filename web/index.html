Threat range

One jump from the staging listed, at maximum skills, with every staging system live and no cyno jammers - which no third-party tool can detect. A second jump reaches further again. Read it as reach, never as a boundary.

FC brief
Report detail

Off by default. When on, and only if the local advisor is running, the brief may carry one sentence naming how the listed systems relate - region, constellation, adjacency or security band. Every word of it is written by this application. The advisor chooses which relation to claim; the claim must be true, and must be true of every system the brief lists, or no sentence is shown at all.
